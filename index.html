The Lost Camel

Unit 2 · Reading 2
Read and answer the questions:

During a family drive through the desert, Saif noticed a young camel standing beside the road. It had one small hump, long legs and a blue rope around its neck. His father stopped the car in a safe place.

“It might be lost,” Saif said.

“It can’t be a wild camel because it has a rope,” replied his sister, Huda. “It must belong to a nearby farm.”

The family stayed inside the car and watched. Suddenly, another vehicle sounded its horn. The frightened camel ran onto the road, and both cars had to stop. Saif’s father called a local animal rescue centre and explained where they were.

“If the camel stays here, there will be an accident,” he told them.

A rescue worker arrived fifteen minutes later. She moved slowly towards the camel and offered it some food. The animal followed her away from the road. Soon, an old farmer came in a truck. He recognised the camel immediately and called it Lulu. He explained that a farm gate had broken during the night, so Lulu had walked away.

The farmer thanked the family and led Lulu into his truck. Before leaving, the rescue worker praised Saif’s father for not trying to catch the frightened animal himself.

“If I ever find another lost animal, I’ll contact an expert again,” Saif decided.
